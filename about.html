About Bright Secondary School

Discover our background, purpose, values and leadership.

Our Origin and Background

Bright Secondary School is a newly established educational institution founded by the Non-Governmental Organization (NGO) SAVE THE CHILD.

The school was established with the aim of creating educational opportunities for learners through academic instruction, practical skills development and supportive learning facilities.

The institution brings together different learning and development facilities, including student dormitories, a computer laboratory, a school farm, a kindergarten section, a vocational training section and an orphanage for children with disabilities.

Bright Secondary School seeks to provide an environment where learners can acquire knowledge, develop their talents and prepare for a productive future.

Our Mission and Vision

Our Mission

To provide inclusive and quality education that empowers learners with academic knowledge, practical skills, good values and opportunities to achieve their full potential.

Our Vision

To become a centre of educational excellence that nurtures responsible, skilled and self-reliant individuals who contribute positively to society.

Our Objectives

  • To provide quality academic education to learners.
  • To promote practical and vocational skills development.
  • To encourage the use of information and communication technology in education.
  • To promote inclusive learning opportunities for children with disabilities.
  • To nurture discipline, responsibility, creativity and good moral values.
  • To equip learners with knowledge and skills that prepare them for future employment and further education.
