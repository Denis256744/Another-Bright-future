Academic Programmes

Building knowledge, developing skills and preparing learners for a successful future.

Our Academic Excellence

Bright Secondary School is committed to supporting learners through quality academic education. Our proposed academic programme covers sciences, languages, humanities, mathematics and technology to promote knowledge, creativity and critical thinking.

Our Approach to Learning

We aim to encourage classroom instruction, practical learning, individual participation, teamwork, research and responsible use of ICT. Our goal is to help learners develop knowledge and skills that can be applied in everyday life.
