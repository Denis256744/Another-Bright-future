School Rules and Regulations

Promoting discipline, responsibility, respect and academic excellence.

Our School Code of Conduct

Bright Secondary School aims to maintain a safe, respectful and supportive environment where every learner can achieve their academic and personal goals.

All students are expected to follow school rules, respect others and take responsibility for their actions.

1. General Conduct and Discipline

  1. Students should respect teachers, school staff, fellow students and visitors.
  2. Students should behave responsibly within the school compound.
  3. Fighting, bullying, harassment and intimidation are prohibited.
  4. Students should avoid abusive, insulting or threatening language.
  5. Students should protect school property and other people's belongings.
  6. Students must follow reasonable instructions from authorised school staff.

2. Attendance and Punctuality

  1. Students should attend all scheduled lessons and school activities.
  2. Students should arrive at school and lessons on time.
  3. Absence from school should be explained by a parent or guardian.
  4. Students who need to leave school during the day should obtain permission.
  5. Students should participate in approved academic and co-curricular activities.

3. Academic Responsibilities

  1. Students should complete assignments and homework on time.
  2. Students should attend lessons with the required learning materials.
  3. Cheating, examination malpractice and plagiarism are prohibited.
  4. Students should respect teachers and allow others to learn without unnecessary interruption.
  5. Students should use the library and computer laboratory responsibly.
  6. Students should take part in tests, examinations and practical learning activities.

4. School Uniform and Personal Hygiene

  1. Students should wear the approved school uniform on designated school days.
  2. Uniforms should be clean, neat and properly maintained.
  3. Students should maintain good personal hygiene.
  4. Students should follow the school's approved grooming guidelines.
  5. Students should dress appropriately during school activities and practical training.

5. Health, Safety and Security

  1. Students should observe safety instructions in classrooms, laboratories and workshops.
  2. Dangerous objects and unauthorised substances must not be brought to school.
  3. Students should report accidents, injuries and unsafe conditions to school staff.
  4. Students should maintain cleanliness in classrooms, dormitories and other facilities.
  5. Students should follow authorised procedures during emergencies.
  6. Students should not leave the school compound without permission.

6. Dormitory Rules

  1. Boarding students should keep dormitories clean and organised.
  2. Students should respect the privacy and property of roommates.
  3. Students should observe approved sleeping and waking times.
  4. Students should not enter dormitories assigned to other groups without permission.
  5. Students should report damaged facilities to the responsible staff member.

7. Computer Laboratory and ICT Rules

  1. Students should use computers only for authorised learning activities.
  2. Food and drinks should not be taken into the computer laboratory.
  3. Students should not install unauthorised software.
  4. Students should not damage, disconnect or interfere with computer equipment.
  5. Students should use the internet responsibly and avoid inappropriate content.
  6. Students should follow the instructions of the ICT teacher or laboratory attendant.

8. School Farm and Vocational Workshop Rules

  1. Students should follow instructions from supervising instructors.
  2. Protective clothing and equipment should be used where required.
  3. Tools and machines should only be operated with permission and proper supervision.
  4. Students should return tools and equipment after use.
  5. Students should observe hygiene and safety procedures during practical activities.

9. Inclusion and Respect for Learners with Disabilities

  1. All students should treat learners with disabilities with dignity and respect.
  2. Discrimination, bullying and exclusion based on disability are prohibited.
  3. Students should respect accessible facilities and learning equipment.
  4. Students should support an inclusive learning environment.
  5. Students should report discrimination or mistreatment to a responsible staff member.

10. School Property and Environment

  1. Students should keep classrooms and the school compound clean.
  2. Destruction or theft of school property is prohibited.
  3. Students should use water and electricity responsibly.
  4. Waste should be disposed of in designated places.
  5. Students should take part in approved environmental conservation activities.

11. Discipline and Corrective Measures

Students who break school rules should be treated fairly and given an opportunity to explain their actions. The school should use appropriate, lawful and non-violent corrective measures.

  • Guidance and counselling.
  • Verbal correction and explanation of expected behaviour.
  • Communication with parents or guardians.
  • Restorative activities where appropriate.
  • Referral to the school administration for serious matters.

Corporal punishment, humiliation, discrimination and other abusive treatment must not be used.

Responsibilities of Parents and Guardians

  • Ensure that children attend school regularly and on time.
  • Provide the necessary learning materials where possible.
  • Communicate with teachers and school administrators about student progress.
  • Attend scheduled parent meetings.
  • Support the school in promoting discipline, safety and good behaviour.
  • Inform the school about relevant learning or accessibility support needs.
Important Notice: These rules are proposed examples prepared for the website project. The school administration and Board of Governors should review and approve the official rules before publication.
