Payment Methods

Bank Payment

Parents may pay school fees through the school's officially designated bank account.

Mobile Money

Mobile money payments may be accepted through an officially approved school payment number.

School Office

Parents may visit the school accounts office for payment guidance and official receipts.

Fees Payment Guidelines

  • Parents and guardians should obtain the official fees structure.
  • Fees should be paid using only officially approved payment channels.
  • Parents should keep all payment receipts for reference.
  • Students should present payment evidence when requested by the accounts office.
  • Parents should contact the school administration if they need clarification about fees.
  • Any bursary or financial assistance arrangement should be confirmed with the school administration.
